How it works

The MedBridge Journey

1

Secure Upload

Patients instantly upload raw, fragmented medical PDFs and clinical notes into their highly secure, encrypted personal dashboard via the Streamlit interface.

2

AI Extraction & Chunking

Under the hood, BioBERT processes complex terminology, chunks the text, and maps it directly into a local vector-space powered by ChromaDB.

3

Conversational RAG

Patients can immediately interact with our LLaMA-based AI Health Assistant, asking pinpoint questions about their deep medical history and receiving cited answers.

4

Instant Clinical Sync

Via cryptographic linking, doctors enter a shared access code and seamlessly ingest the mapped records directly into their own clinical panel view instantly.

Features

A Modern Healthcare Stack.

AI Document Analysis

Upload raw medical PDFs and let our secure RAG pipelines generate instant, plain-English summaries of complex medical terminology. We process text through BioBERT to ensure clinical-grade precision.

Automated Risk Scoring

Real-time population analytics automatically detect high risks for cardiovascular and kidney conditions.

Health Chat

Ask "When was my last A1C?" and get cited answers instantly.

Follow-Ups

AI automatically tracks required post-visit appointments.

Cryptographic Sharing

Patients generate secure, time-limited access codes. Doctors use them to immediately sync the patient into their clinical panel.
